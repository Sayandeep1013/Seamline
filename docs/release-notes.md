**Gravity that behaves.** The ball now has real momentum. It speeds up going down (gently capped), slows going up, and rolls back when it runs out of speed. Every bounce loses energy, so after a miss it settles where gravity puts it instead of climbing back up a pillar.

- **Hold** is rebuilt around the same idea (one beam, two jobs) with no climbing. The beam bridges the ball to the cradle; slide it, tap, and the ball curls back along a U and crosses the beam a second time to the bowl
- **The Star** now looks like a star: four slim blades. The ball stays visible as it rides a blade, and only real end-to-end joins glow, as a single fine line
