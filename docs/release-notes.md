**Nothing passes through anything.** The ball now collides with exactly what you see. It can't slip through an edge, roll behind a piece in front of it, or vanish mid-fall. Every frame of every possible move in every level is checked automatically before a build ships.

- **The Star** is now one solid four-pointed star. The ball rides inside one of its curved faces, so it never passes through a point. At the right height the pillar flows through the star into the goal floor; one step too low and the ball knocks against the floor's edge
- **The start gate** is a small bar ahead of the ball that slides back into its post when you tap, so it never overlaps the ball
- Falling balls stay in view and land on the first floor beneath them
- A piece can't be dragged across the ball, and the world won't turn to a view that hides it
- The turning knob on curved pieces is now a small hub at the centre, so it never covers the ball
